Questions

Straight answers.

What kind of buildings do you work on?

Light commercial is the core — restaurants, breweries, retail strips, offices, dental and auto shops, typically rooftop units from 2 to 25 tons. We also handle residential service, repair and system replacement. Not large industrial plants or new construction.

Do you offer maintenance agreements?

Yes. Quarterly or semi-annual schedules with documented reports each visit, written per site after a walkthrough of your actual equipment.

Are you licensed and insured?

Kelvex operates as Fix It 44 LLC and carries general liability insurance. Certificate of insurance naming your property available on request. EPA 608 certified for refrigerant handling.

How does pricing work?

Flat trip and diagnostic rate for the visit. Repairs quoted in writing before work begins, parts and labor itemized. Maintenance priced per unit, per visit. See pricing →

Do you service evaporative coolers?

Yes, and it's core work here. Pumps, media, sump service, water circuit repair, and the fall winterization that prevents the freeze damage that kills these units every winter.
