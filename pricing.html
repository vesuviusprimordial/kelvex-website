What is included in a quote

  • Itemized parts and labor for each line of work
  • Written condition notes explaining why the work is needed
  • Clear exclusions — what the quote does not cover
  • A total, valid for 30 days from the issue date

Change orders

If we open a unit and find something the quote did not cover, work stops and you get a revised quote. Additional work is never performed and billed without your written approval.

Payment

  • Payment is due on receipt of invoice unless other terms are agreed in writing.
  • We accept credit and debit cards, ACH bank transfer, and check.
  • Card payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your card details.
  • Commercial accounts may request net terms, subject to approval.
  • Deposits may be required on jobs with significant parts cost. Any deposit is stated on the quote.

Warranty

Parts we supply carry the manufacturer's warranty. Our labor is warranted for 30 days from the date of service on the specific repair performed. Warranty does not cover unrelated failures, pre-existing conditions noted as excluded on the quote, or damage from lack of maintenance, freeze, or work performed by others.
